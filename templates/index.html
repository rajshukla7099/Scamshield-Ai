    🛡️ Essential Citizen Guide

    Safety Tips for Indian Internet Users

    Practical knowledge to protect against 95% of everyday digital scams in India.

    📲 1. The UPI Golden Law

    Entering your UPI PIN ALWAYS deducts money. You NEVER need to enter your PIN or scan a QR code to RECEIVE money.

    🔐 2. The OTP Sacred Principle

    Legitimate bank employees, police officers, and electricity boards will NEVER ask for an OTP or NetBanking password. OTP disclosure = account compromise.

    ⚖️ 3. 'Digital Arrest' is 100% Fake

    There is NO legal concept of Digital Arrest under Indian law. Police, CBI, ED, and courts NEVER conduct interrogations or demand bail over WhatsApp or Skype.

    ⚡ 4. The Electricity Bill Rule

    State power distribution companies never disconnect power within 2 hours via personal SMS or instruct you to call an individual 10-digit mobile number.

    📲 5. Never Sideload APK Files

    Never install AnyDesk, QuickSupport, or download `.apk` files sent by strangers. These allow scammers to view your screen, read incoming OTPs, and drain accounts.

    📞 6. Fake Customer Care Numbers

    Never search for customer support phone numbers on Google or social media. Scammers manipulate search results. Always use the official help section inside the app.

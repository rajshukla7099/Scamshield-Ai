Project Objective & Mission
AI-assisted heuristic defense democratizing cyber safety across Digital Bharat

ScamShield AI was conceptualized and engineered for HYPNEXIS ’26 at Manipal University Jaipur as an offline-first, explainable digital safety web application. In India, cyber fraud escalated past ₹7,000 Crores in 2024, disproportionately targeting students, senior citizens, and first-time digital payment users through social engineering, Hinglish SMS, lookalike URLs, and fake authority impersonation.

Unlike opaque closed-source threat intelligence feeds or paid third-party APIs, ScamShield AI operates with a 100% local, explainable heuristic engine. It explains exactly why a message or link triggers suspicion, highlights predatory phrases, and provides actionable incident response steps.

⚙️ How the Detection Engine Works

Our modular Python rule engine uses defense-in-depth lexical analysis, mathematical entropy, and contextual NLP:

1. URL Lexical & TLD Reputation

Inspects scheme encryption (HTTP vs HTTPS), flags raw numerical IPv4/IPv6 hosts, flags 25+ disposable high-risk TLDs (.xyz, .top, .icu), detects link shortening concealment (bit.ly, t.co), and catches @ symbol authority spoofing.

2. Brand Typosquatting & Entropy

Computes Levenshtein edit distance against 40+ official brand domains (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Paytm, India Post) alongside Shannon entropy formulas to catch algorithmic domain generation (DGAs) and deceptive homoglyph characters.

3. Multilingual & Hinglish NLP Matrix

Custom contextual regex patterns detect psychological pressure and colloquial deception across 12 distinct Indian fraud taxonomies (Digital Arrest, Electricity Cut, Telegram VIP tasks, and UPI Reverse PIN traps).

4. Negation-Aware Scoring

Contextual window checks prevent false positives. Security advisories like "Never share your OTP" or "Bank will not ask for PIN" are recognized as legitimate safety notices rather than fraud attempts.

🇮🇳 Intended Social Impact & Target Audience

Engineered specifically to protect vulnerable user demographics across India:

College Students & Youth

Shields students from pre-paid Telegram work-from-home tasks, fake scholarship links, fraudulent exam portals, and student loan advance fee scams.

Senior Citizens & Retirees

Protects elderly citizens from terrifying 'Digital Arrest' video calls, urgent electricity power disconnection threats, and fake pension KYC update SMS.

Digital Payment & UPI Users

Educates users against reverse QR code traps where victims mistakenly enter their UPI PIN expecting to receive money, instead of transferring money out.

Online Shoppers & Small Merchants

Warns shoppers of fake India Post redelivery links, spoofed Swiggy/Zomato refund calls, and counterfeit e-commerce discounts.

⚖️ System Limitations & Privacy Architecture

  • Zero Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF): ScamShield AI NEVER automatically visits, fetches, or downloads files from user-submitted URLs. Analysis is performed strictly offline on lexical strings and metadata to guarantee server security.
  • Privacy by Design: We never solicit, store, or transmit OTPs, passwords, ATM PINs, or NetBanking credentials. All phone numbers and email addresses are automatically masked before any audit logging.
  • Heuristic Assessment, Not a Formal Warranty: Scam detection is inherently probabilistic. A Low Risk score indicates no red flags were detected by our current rule matrices; it does NOT constitute an absolute legal guarantee of safety.
  • Independent Educational Tool: ScamShield AI is an independent academic innovation built for HYPNEXIS ’26. It operates autonomously and does not claim direct real-time integration with classified law enforcement databases.

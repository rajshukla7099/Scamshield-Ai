6 Major Scam Vectors & Practical Defense Protocols

Detailed analysis of the top fraud methods targeting Indian digital users and concrete safety actions.

🔗

1. Phishing Links & Lookalike Domains

Category: Credential Theft & Impersonation

How it works: Scammers register lookalike domains using typosquatting (e.g. paypa1.com or sbi-kyc-update.xyz) or URL shorteners (bit.ly) to clone legitimate banking and shopping portals. When victims enter credentials, passwords and OTPs are relayed directly to the attacker.

Red Flag Indicators: Free/cheap TLDs (.xyz, .top, .icu), raw IP addresses in address bar, excessive hyphens, unencrypted HTTP, or misspellings in the brand name.
Practical Safety Recommendations:
  • Always bookmark verified official portals (e.g., https://www.onlinesbi.sbi) rather than clicking links in SMS.
  • Government portals strictly end in .gov.in or .nic.in. Never trust .com or .org claims for Indian government schemes.
  • Use ScamShield AI to inspect unknown URLs before submitting passwords or personal info.
💼

2. Fake Job Offers & Telegram Task Scams

Category: Advance-Fee & Ponzi Traps

How it works: Victims receive unsolicited WhatsApp or Telegram messages offering work-from-home jobs paying ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 daily for trivial tasks like liking YouTube videos or rating Google Maps hotels. After paying small initial bonuses (₹200-₹500) to build trust, scammers demand "VIP deposits" or "cryptocurrency recharge" before locking victims out.

Red Flag Indicators: Guaranteed daily income for zero skills, communication shifted to anonymous Telegram channels, demands for security deposits or prepaid tasks.
Practical Safety Recommendations:
  • Remember: Legitimate employers NEVER ask candidates to pay money for training, equipment, or "VIP level unlocks".
  • Verify job openings on the company's verified LinkedIn or official careers page.
  • Immediately exit and report any Telegram or WhatsApp groups conducting prepaid tasks.
📲

3. OTP & UPI Social Engineering (Reverse PIN Traps)

Category: Direct Financial Depletion

How it works: Attackers send a "Collect Request" on PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm disguised as a "Cashback / Refund" or send a QR code claiming: "Scan this QR code and enter your PIN to receive money". Alternatively, they call impersonating bank staff and demand the 6-digit OTP to "stop unauthorized charges".

Red Flag Indicators: Being asked to enter UPI PIN to receive money; calls claiming an OTP is needed to cancel an imaginary charge.
Practical Safety Recommendations:
  • The Golden UPI Law: Entering your PIN always deducts money. Money sent to you arrives automatically without any PIN or QR scan.
  • Never share an OTP with anyone under any circumstances, even if caller claims to be bank manager or police officer.
  • In case of accidental transfer, dial 1930 immediately within the initial Golden Hour.
📞

4. Fake Customer Care Numbers & Search Engine Poisoning

Category: Helpdesk Interception

How it works: When users search on Google for "Swiggy refund helpline", "SBI customer care", or "Airlines baggage support", scammers upload fake Google Maps listings or optimized ads containing personal 10-digit mobile numbers. When victims call, fraudsters instruct them to install AnyDesk/QuickSupport or send ₹1 to "verify account".

Red Flag Indicators: Standard 10-digit mobile numbers listed as customer care for major corporations; representative asks you to install remote sharing tools.
Practical Safety Recommendations:
  • Never search for customer support phone numbers on Google or social media.
  • Strictly use the verified "Help / Support" chat feature located directly inside the official mobile app.
  • Banks and delivery apps will never ask you to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport.
🎁

5. Lottery, KBC & Free Prize Claims

Category: Advance Fee Fraud

How it works: Victims receive an audio clip or letter claiming they won ₹25,00,000 in Kaun Banega Crorepati (KBC), a free Mahindra Thar car, or an Amazon shopping voucher. To claim the prize, victims are told to transfer "refundable registration fees", "GST tax", or "processing charges". Once paid, the fraudster disappears.

Red Flag Indicators: Winning a contest or lottery you never entered; audio notes sent via WhatsApp with fake Amitabh Bachchan voice or forged RBI letters.
Practical Safety Recommendations:
  • Remember the universal truth: You cannot win a contest or lottery you never participated in.
  • No genuine organization requires winners to pay fees or taxes in advance into individual bank accounts.
  • Block and report these WhatsApp numbers immediately.
⚖️

6. Impersonation & Digital Arrest Extortion

Category: Psychological Coercion & Panic

How it works: Attackers impersonate police officers, CBI agents, Customs officials, or electricity board officers. They create intense panic by claiming you sent a parcel containing illegal narcotics or that your electricity will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM. In "Digital Arrest", they force victims onto continuous Skype video calls while coercing them to transfer savings into "RBI escrow accounts".

Red Flag Indicators: Demands to stay on continuous video call; claims of 'Digital Arrest'; instructions to transfer funds to 'verify clean money'.
Practical Safety Recommendations:
  • Digital Arrest DOES NOT EXIST in Indian law. No police officer or court ever interrogates or arrests anyone over Skype or WhatsApp.
  • Disconnect the call immediately. If genuine law enforcement requires you, they will serve an official written summons under CrPC/BNSS.
  • Immediately report extortion calls to your local police station or dial 1930.

🚨 Immediate Incident Response Protocol (Golden Hour)

If you realize you sent money to a scammer or disclosed your credentials within the past 2 hours:

  1. Dial 1930 Immediately: The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal helpline works with banks to halt transactions in transit.
  2. Lock Cards & UPI: Open your official mobile banking app and freeze all debit/credit cards and reset your UPI PIN.
  3. File Official Complaint: Submit transaction IDs, screenshots, and beneficiary account numbers at cybercrime.gov.in ↗.
  4. Notify Your Bank: Formally notify your bank branch manager in writing within 3 working days for zero-liability protection under RBI guidelines.

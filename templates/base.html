🛡️ ScamShield AI — Platform Overview & Architecture

1. The Problem

Over ₹7,000 Crores were lost to digital financial frauds in India in 2024 alone. Attackers exploit high urgency, WhatsApp messages, Hinglish social engineering, and lookalike domains (SBI, Paytm, India Post) to deceive citizens into disclosing OTPs or UPI PINs.

2. Our Solution

ScamShield AI provides an intelligent, explainable scam detection ecosystem combining URL Heuristics (typosquatting, high-risk TLDs, raw IP hosts) and a Multilingual/Hinglish NLP Rule Engine that highlights threat phrases in real-time, completely offline without requiring paid external APIs.

3. Key Innovations

  • Multilingual & Hinglish NLP: Understands "Bijli kat jayega", "Ghar baithe paise kamaye", and "Digital Arrest" scams.
  • Explainable Heuristics: Transparent scoring (0-100) with character-level span highlighting, explaining exactly *why* a message is dangerous.
  • Zero-Cost & Offline-Ready: Works out-of-the-box with built-in heuristic intelligence; optional LLM integration.
  • Community Radar & Defense Academy: Crowd-sourced threat logging with PII masking and interactive training scenarios.

4. Quick Feature Guide

  1. Open URL Scanner ➔ Test links for typosquatting, high-risk TLDs, and IP hosts.
  2. Open Message Analyzer ➔ See real-time threat highlighting on deceptive SMS & WhatsApp messages.
  3. View Dashboard with real-time risk analytics and security telemetry.
  4. Practice scam detection in Scam Academy with interactive quiz scenarios.
